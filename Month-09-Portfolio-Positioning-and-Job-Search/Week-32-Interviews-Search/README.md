## 🎤 Interview drill

**Q — What would you do in your first 90 days as our IAM engineer?**

> Days 1–30: inventory identities (human + NHI), map current access and quick wins (MFA gaps, standing admin). Days 31–60: close top risks — phishing-resistant MFA for admins, PIM/JIT, kill legacy auth. Days 61–90: automate JML and access reviews, stand up detections, and report progress mapped to the control framework.
